KnowMore Reading Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 September 2026

1. Introduction

KnowMore Reading (“KnowMore”, “we”, “us” or “our”) is a speed-reading and reading-comprehension app. This Privacy Policy explains what information KnowMore processes, how it is used, when information may be sent to third-party service providers, and the choices available to you.

KnowMore is designed to minimise data collection. Most app data is stored locally on your device.

2. Information stored locally on your device

KnowMore may store the following information locally on your device:

  • Imported PDF files and extracted reading content.
  • Reading position, reading history, reading sessions and progress.
  • Reading speed, words-per-flash, text-size and reading-focus preferences.
  • Quiz attempts, quiz scores and comprehension results.
  • KnowMore Score, streaks, milestones and related progress information.
  • Profile information you choose to provide, such as first name, age or age range, date of birth and reading motivations.
  • App preferences, including appearance, haptics, sounds and daily reading goals.
  • Approximate location information used to calculate local sunrise and sunset for the Home screen environment.

This locally stored information is not used for advertising or cross-app tracking.

3. Imported documents

Imported PDF documents are primarily stored and processed on your device.

KnowMore does not automatically upload an entire imported PDF simply because you add it to your library.

If you choose to use an AI-powered quiz feature, the text from the relevant chapter or section may be sent off-device as described below.

4. AI-powered quiz generation

When you request an AI-generated quiz, KnowMore may send the following information securely over HTTPS to the KnowMore backend:

  • The relevant chapter or section text.
  • Book and chapter titles.
  • Random technical document and chapter identifiers.
  • Information needed to avoid repeating quiz questions.
  • An optional age value, where available, so quiz difficulty can be better matched to the reader.

This information is used only to provide the requested quiz functionality, maintain service reliability and protect the service from misuse.

KnowMore does not use this information for advertising or cross-app tracking.

5. Third-party service providers

KnowMore uses third-party service providers to provide AI-powered quiz functionality.

Render. KnowMore uses Render to host the secure backend that receives quiz-generation requests and communicates with the AI service.

OpenAI. KnowMore uses the OpenAI API to analyse relevant reading content and generate quiz questions and related responses. This means content submitted for an AI quiz may be processed by OpenAI on KnowMore’s behalf.

OpenAI states that API inputs and outputs are not used to train its models by default unless the API customer explicitly opts in to data sharing.

We require service providers that process data on our behalf to handle it only for the relevant service purpose and subject to their applicable privacy and security obligations.

6. Approximate location

KnowMore may request permission to access your approximate location.

Location is used only to calculate local sunrise and sunset times so that the Home screen can reflect the local time of day.

Your approximate location is stored locally on your device for this purpose and is not sent to KnowMore’s quiz backend, OpenAI or advertising services.

You can revoke location access at any time through iOS Settings.

7. Data we do not use for advertising or tracking

KnowMore does not use your reading content, profile information, location or quiz activity to track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.

KnowMore does not sell your personal information.

KnowMore does not operate an advertising network or use your data to deliver targeted advertising.

8. Data retention

Local data. Information stored locally on your device remains there until you remove the relevant content, reset applicable app data, or delete the app, subject to normal iOS backup behaviour.

Imported documents. Deleting an imported document from KnowMore removes the app’s local copy and associated local reading record where supported by the app.

AI quiz requests. KnowMore is designed not to use submitted chapter content to create advertising profiles or identity-based user profiles. Data submitted to backend and AI service providers may be processed or temporarily retained as necessary to provide, secure and operate those services, subject to the providers’ applicable retention policies and KnowMore’s service configuration.

9. Deleting or changing your information

You can manage or remove much of your locally stored information directly in KnowMore, including imported documents and profile information.

You can also delete the app from your device to remove app data stored locally, subject to information retained in device backups controlled by you or Apple.

For questions or requests concerning information processed through KnowMore’s backend, contact [SUPPORT EMAIL].

We may need sufficient information to identify the relevant request without asking for more personal information than necessary.

11. Security

KnowMore uses reasonable technical measures intended to protect information processed by the service. Quiz-generation requests are transmitted using HTTPS.

No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

12. Children and younger users

KnowMore is a general-audience reading app. We do not intentionally use personal information from younger users for advertising or tracking.

If local law requires parental or guardian consent for a younger user to provide information to an online service, the app should be used with the appropriate permission.

13. International processing

Our service providers may process information in countries other than the country in which you live. Where required, appropriate safeguards are used for international transfers of personal data.

14. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when KnowMore’s features, service providers or legal obligations change.

When we make material changes, we will update the “Last updated” date and, where appropriate, provide additional notice.
